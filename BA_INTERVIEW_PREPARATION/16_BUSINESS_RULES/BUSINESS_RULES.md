## 1. Executive Overview

Business Rules define the operational boundaries, validation constraints, calculations, and security safeguards of an enterprise system. In BAHub, business rules are implemented across three architectural tiers:
1.  **Database Layer Constraints:** Unique indexes, cascade behaviors, and model `save()` triggers.
2.  **API / Middleware Layer:** Subscription verification, seat quota checks, role permissions (`IsEnterprise`), and validation serializers.
3.  **Application Domain Layer:** Workflows for document sign-off immutability, diagram locks, and Gherkin formatting.

| **BRULE-014** | **UAT Defect Severity Governance:** A test case cannot achieve final `PASSED` verification status while an associated `CRITICAL` or `HIGH` defect remains in `OPEN` or `IN_PROGRESS` status. | Prevents software delivery teams from signing off on releases with known high-severity bugs. | `backend/uat/models.py:TestCase`, `Defect` | UAT & Quality | Workarounds documented via approved `ChangeRequest` can downgrade defect severity with PO approval. |
| **BRULE-015** | **Soft Delete Data Retention:** Invoking `.delete()` on any business entity must set `is_deleted=True` and update audit timestamps without deleting the relational database row. | Ensures zero accidental data loss and maintains historical auditability for SOC 2 compliance. | `backend/core/models.py:BaseModel.delete()` (lines 40–44) | Core Platform | Hard deletes (`.hard_delete()`) are restricted to automated database cleanup scripts (`seed_rich_demo_data.py`). |
